1. Scout: Gather Useful Information

Before the battle, do not collect random information. Focus on details that change how you fight.

Enemy Analysis

  • Element weaknesses
  • Status weaknesses
  • Breakable parts
  • Attack patterns
  • Fast or slow movement
  • Ground or airborne attacks

Battle Phases

  • Normal state
  • Enraged state
  • Final phase
  • Emergency attacks
  • Environment changes

Win Condition

  • Slay the monster
  • Capture the monster
  • Break a specific part
  • Survive the timer
  • Protect an objective

2. Plan: Build Your Battle Setup

Set a Clear Goal

Example: “I want to capture the monster and break its head before the quest ends.”

Goal Capture Part Break

Pre-Battle Checklist

  • Correct weapon
  • Element resistance
  • Healing items
  • Buff items
  • Traps and tranq bombs
  • Food buffs

Priority Order

  1. Stay alive
  2. Learn dangerous attacks
  3. Break weak parts
  4. Maximize damage
Important: Many players accidentally make damage their first priority. In hard fights, survival and pattern recognition come first.

3. Execute: Make Better Decisions During Combat

Weak Approach

“The guide said dodge left after this attack, so I only look for that exact move.”

Better Approach

“Heavy attacks usually create safe openings, so I should punish after the monster finishes the move.”

Combat Decision Loop

  1. Can I safely attack?
  2. If yes, choose a short or long combo.
  3. If no, reposition, heal, sharpen, or wait.
  4. Observe the monster's next move.
  5. Repeat the loop.

4. Adapt: Change the Plan When Needed

If You Are Running Out of Healing

Play safer, stop forcing attacks, and only heal after the monster finishes a big move.

If You Keep Getting Hit

Focus on dodging for one minute without attacking. Learn the monster's timing first.

If You Lack Damage

Target weak parts, upgrade your weapon, use buffs, and punish longer recovery windows.

5. Review: Improve After Losing

Do not just say the boss is unfair. Find the exact reason you lost and fix one thing at a time.

After-Death Questions

  • What attack killed me?
  • Why was I standing there?
  • Was I being greedy?
  • Did I panic heal?
  • Did I ignore a phase change?
  • Was my gear wrong for the fight?

6. Strategy

7. Rewards
